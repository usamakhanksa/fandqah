How to choose

Choose on compliance, not on room count

Most properties pick the wrong package because they count rooms. The better question is which authorities you must file data to.

One property under 10 units

Start on Starter. It is free and covers front desk, reservations and basic reports.

One property up to 100 units

Core adds housekeeping, invoicing, revenue management and the booking engine.

You need Shomoos and Tourism, or several properties

Connect. It adds government filing, central reservations and POS integration.

You need ZATCA, a website and channel connectivity

Pro. It adds Phase 2 e-invoicing, your website and booking-site connectivity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Fandaqah cost?
There are four annual packages: Starter is free (1 user, up to 10 units), Core is SAR 1,200, Connect is SAR 1,980 and Pro is SAR 3,000. Prices exclude VAT, and a one-time SAR 300 setup fee plus 15% VAT are added.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. You can register for a free trial before subscribing, with no credit card required.
Is the setup fee recurring?
No. The SAR 300 setup fee is charged once, at onboarding only.
Are there per-user fees?
Starter allows one user. Core, Connect and Pro have flexible user counts: the package is decided by unit count and the government integrations you need, not by users.
