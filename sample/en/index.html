Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fandaqah?
Fandaqah is a Saudi cloud system for managing hotels, serviced apartments and resorts. It combines a property management system (PMS), a channel manager and a direct booking engine in one platform, with native integration to ZATCA, the Ministry of Tourism and the Shomoos platform. More than 800 properties in the Kingdom run on it.
Is Fandaqah compliant with ZATCA e-invoicing?
Yes. Fandaqah is compliant with ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing. VAT at 15% is calculated automatically on every invoice, and invoices are filed to the authority's platform with no manual steps.
Does Fandaqah integrate with Shomoos and the Ministry of Tourism?
Yes. The system registers guest data with the Shomoos platform and files occupancy data to the Ministry of Tourism's National Tourism Monitoring Platform automatically, plus disclosure reports for the Balady platform.
How long does it take to go live?
Onboarding starts with a one-time setup fee of SAR 300 and covers configuration, channel connection and team training. A free trial is available before you subscribe.
Is the system available in Arabic and English?
Yes. The interface, reports and technical support are fully available in Arabic and English, with 24/7 multilingual guest messaging.
