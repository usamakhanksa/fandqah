Good to know

A few questions, answered.

Want to understand how these modules fit your property? Start here, then request a walkthrough tailored to your operation.

What can the housekeeping module help us coordinate?+
The intended workflow covers room status visibility, cleaning assignments, task tracking and inspections. The Fandaqah team can confirm the available configuration for your property.
What inventory workflows can we review?+
The feature walkthrough can cover item lists, current and minimum stock, low-stock visibility, and receiving or issuing supplies, subject to your enabled module configuration.
Can maintenance requests be prioritized and scheduled?+
The proposed workflow demonstrates work orders, priority, assignment, scheduling and completion tracking. Ask the team to confirm which controls are available in your PMS plan.
Is this suitable for serviced apartments and chalets?+
Fandaqah serves hospitality operators including hotels and serviced apartments. Share your property type and workflow needs so the team can assess fit, including chalets and holiday rentals.
How do I see these features on my property setup?+
Send a demo request through the form. It opens a prefilled WhatsApp message for you to review and send to the Fandaqah team. The form does not store your details on this webpage.
