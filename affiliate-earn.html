Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

Is the partnership program free?

Yes: It's 100% free to join — no setup fees, no monthly fees. You can start earning commissions immediately after signing up

How and when do I receive my commission?

You can request a withdrawal anytime once your balance reaches a minimum of 100 SAR. We process payments within 3–5 business days via bank transfer or STC Pay. You can select your preferred payment method in your dashboard.

How do I track my referrals and earnings?

Get access to a comprehensive dashboard where you can track registrations, conversion rates, earned commissions, and payment history with all data updated in real time.

Can I refer hotels from any country?

Currently, our affiliate program focuses on hotels within Saudi Arabia and GCC countries. We're continuously expanding our reach and will update affiliates when we open up to more regions.

What marketing materials do you provide?

We provide a complete marketing kit including: banners in various sizes, email templates, social media posts, brochures, case studies, and product demos. All materials are regularly updated and available in both English and Arabic.
